Reference 3U CubeSat architecture. Synthetic teaching fixture; not a real mission.
The 3U CubeSat contains the Payload, Power, Communication, Attitude control, Avionics, Structure and Thermal subsystems.
Camera CAM-3U mass is 350 g. Operating power is 4.5 W. Peak current is 1.4 A. Nominal voltage is 3.3 V. Thickness along the stack is 22 mm.

Radiator panel mass is 90 g.
Payload allocated mass is the sum of the Camera, Optics barrel and Payload storage board masses.
Total mass is the sum of the Camera, Optics barrel, Payload storage board, Battery pack, EPS board, Solar array, UHF transceiver, Deployable antenna, Reaction wheel, Magnetorquer set, On-board computer, 3U chassis and Radiator panel masses.
Payload stack height is the sum of the Camera, Optics barrel and Deployable antenna thicknesses.
Orbit average power is the sum of the Camera operating power, the UHF transceiver average power, the Reaction wheel operating power, the Magnetorquer set operating power, the On-board computer operating power and the Payload storage board operating power.
UHF transceiver average power is its transmit power during the transmit duty cycle plus its receive power for the rest of the orbit.
Power margin is the Solar array generated power minus the Orbit average power.
Autonomy in minutes is the Battery pack available energy in Wh divided by the Orbit average power in W, multiplied by 60.
The Camera and the Optics barrel are mounted on the 3U chassis; their mass drives the launch load the chassis must carry and the position of the centre of mass.
The attitude control authority depends on the mass distribution of the Payload; the Reaction wheel is sized from the moment of inertia and the centre of mass offset.
The Camera is thermally coupled to the Radiator panel; its dissipated power sets the radiator area.
The EPS board powers the Camera, the UHF transceiver and the Reaction wheel; each supply line is limited by the EPS continuous output.
