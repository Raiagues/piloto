# Extraction experiment protocol — revision 1

Registered 8 September 2026, after checkpoint `6dd8639` and product audit `570d20d`, before the final acceptance series. Exploratory results and all previous failed attempts remain retained under ignored `var/benchmarks/`. The evaluator freeze `v0.1-development.1` is unchanged.

## Acceptance fixed before final runs

The final selected implementation must produce at least **five provider-completed end-to-end extractions**, with final-contract validity and complete A0 each **at least 80% of completed extractions**. All evaluated outputs must have no hierarchy cycles, no evaluator leakage and no unsupported documented evidence. Invalid outputs never become persisted baselines. Also report usable baselines divided by **all** attempted extractions, every physical stage request, and provider completion separately. A multi-stage extraction is completed only if every required provider stage returns a complete public response. JSON/schema failures remain in that denominator.

Run five fresh extractions, extending to at most ten only to obtain five completed extractions when transport failures intervene. Do not drop an output, retry a semantic failure inside a run, reuse a prior result, select the best five, or move thresholds. If the threshold fails, retain the entire series, make a justified implementation revision and register a new full series. A persistent external blocker requires evidence from reasonable alternative models, smaller requests and bounded retries.

80% is an **explicit development acceptance target requested by the owner**, not an industry reliability standard or a statistical proof. Four successes among five has a very wide binomial uncertainty interval and cannot establish production-grade reliability. No source establishes 80% as adequate for engineering certification. Independent human engineering review remains pending regardless of automated results.

## Official research and implementation decisions

Accessed 8 September 2026:

- [Gemini troubleshooting](https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/docs/troubleshooting): direct REST callers should use bounded exponential backoff with jitter for transient failures. Norte's candidate policy allows two physical requests per stage, 1-second initial backoff with jitter, respects Retry-After, 90-second per-request deadline and 185-second total stage budget. No retry for malformed content, schema/semantic rejection, 400/401/403/404; transient 408/429/500/502/503/504 and transport timeout are eligible. A long Retry-After exceeding the remaining budget ends the operation instead of retrying prematurely. These exact limits are our UX choice, not provider-prescribed constants.
- [API errors](https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/docs/api-errors): 503 denotes temporary service unavailability; 504 is a provider deadline. A local AbortSignal timeout is recorded separately. Aborting the HTTP client does **not** prove that provider computation stopped; this integration has no server-side cancellation acknowledgement or processing-state endpoint. Retrying may perform duplicate billed computation, though baseline persistence remains atomic and coalesced.
- [Rate limits](https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/docs/rate-limits): limits apply per project and vary with model/tier; actual limits require the account dashboard. A 503 is not evidence of exhausted quota. Do not infer account quota from the public catalogue. Record only whitelisted returned timing/request/rate-limit headers; absent values stay unknown.
- [Structured outputs](https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/docs/structured-output): supported JSON Schema is a subset and deeply nested/large schemas may be rejected. Schema shape is not evidence verification. We measure schema characters, request bytes, source-context characters, public response bytes, time to response headers and full latency. The non-streaming endpoint does not expose token-level time-to-first-token. Character-count/4 is explicitly approximate; usageMetadata is the separate actual provider token accounting when returned.
- [Models](https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/docs/models), [3.5 Flash-Lite](https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/docs/models/gemini-3.5-flash-lite), [3.8 Flash](https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/docs/models/gemini-3.8-flash): both are stable structured-output candidates. Lite emphasizes low latency/cost and simple extraction; Flash targets more complex workflows. Published positioning does not establish accuracy or availability on Norte; measure both. Both advertise million-token input context, far above this curated pack. A newer model is not selected solely because it is newer.
- [Pricing](https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/docs/pricing): standard Flash-Lite text input/output is listed at $0.30/$2.50 per million tokens. Record the exact model, tier and token counts before estimating cost; output includes thinking usage. No account billing settings are changed.
- [REST GenerationConfig](https://ai.google.dev/api/generate-content#v1beta.GenerationConfig): `seed` is an optional decoding seed; omission uses a random seed. Temperature 0.1 and maxOutputTokens 16,000 are recorded; other settings remain provider defaults unless explicitly logged. Hidden reasoning is never retained or exposed.

Evidence-first assembly separates source capture from interpretation. Its benefit is an engineering hypothesis to be measured, not a claim supported by the structured-output documentation. Relation directions use generic examples only; quantitative completeness rules are general, with no mission-specific property mandate. The full relation vocabulary remains in persistence for existing models; extraction omits `contains` because parentId already carries hierarchy. Reducing the other kinds is deferred until observed ambiguity justifies it.

## Follow-up hypotheses and final-series registration

Exploration exposed fact loss before assembly and formula expressions emitted where an enum identifier was required. Two further alternatives retain a deterministic ledger of all literal source fragments, first with explicit calculation input handles and then with a typed formula declaration. Code derives dependency direction, never numerical calculation results. These alternatives remain experimental: they did not establish acceptable complete extraction. Their stage snapshots preserve source IDs and classification; they are not enabled in the product.

[Microsoft GraphRAG's dataflow](https://microsoft.github.io/graphrag/index/default_dataflow/) retains document/text-unit links and separates graph extraction from optional claims. This supports examining source units and stages as an architectural pattern, not a measured reliability claim for Norte. Its graph clustering and co-occurrence methods are not imported into physical reasoning. [PROV-O](https://www.w3.org/TR/prov-o/) distinguishes entities, generating activities and derivation; Norte uses lightweight source/stage records without claiming ontology compliance. [A structural extraction study](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.10881) evaluates progressively more relational/numerical schemas in evidence synthesis, supporting diagnostic ablation as a method; results from that domain are not Norte accuracy estimates.

The current [Gemini thinking guide](https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/docs/generate-content/thinking) documents minimal default effort for Lite and medium for 3.5 Flash. A two-run/model exploratory comparison explicitly used `thinkingLevel=low`, still temperature 0.1, on the reduced monolithic contract. It produced no complete A0 pass. The [3.5 migration guide](https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/docs/generate-content/whats-new-gemini-3.5) recommends default sampling settings, but this investigation retains the owner's explicit low-temperature constraint rather than silently changing temperature to the provider default. No private thoughts or signatures were retained. A 64-token minimal provider control returned a truncated response for one model; this demonstrates a response, not successful engineering extraction or a valid JSON control.

**Final registration (before fresh acceptance requests):** retain the default `gemini-3.5-flash-lite`, temperature 0.1, maxOutputTokens 16,000 and provider-default thinking; use the production monolithic prompt with one parent hierarchy and deterministic metadata hydration. Repeated staged alternatives have not shown a better accepted baseline, so do not promote them. The literal ledger and typed-calculation variants remain available to reproduce the experiments. The production model, input handles and relation vocabulary remain compatible with current reasoning. No engine arithmetic, frozen evaluator aliases, tolerances, requirements, expected relations or context facts were changed.

Run `diagnose:extraction -- --strategy A --models gemini-3.5-flash-lite --runs 10 --acceptance true --output-dir NEW_DIRECTORY`. Stop once five extractions have provider-completed, or after ten attempted extractions, whichever occurs first. Every failed response remains in its proper denominator. The unchanged thresholds at the top of this document apply, including A0 completeness; this registration is not a prediction that they will pass. The manifest hashes the production sources, exact request and evaluator freeze before generation.
